# Summer Project — Strategy & Method (v0)

*Living document. Started 2026-07-15. We edit this as we learn. It is the north star, not a pitch.*

## The mission (reframed)
Find a **mis-served** market with acute, frequent, expensive pain — not an "untouched" one (untouched + big + painful is usually a contradiction). Then build the smallest thing people will pay for, and only then scale.

## Decisions (2026-07-15)
- **Start from:** Måns's *real, demonstrated* edge (see below) — not pricing, which is aspirational until SK starts.
- **Build:** can go deep via AI coding (Claude Code). Constraint isn't complexity — it's maintenance burden + the fact that code is never the moat.
- **Lens:** keep venture-scale and cash-flow side business both open.
- **Capital at risk before revenue:** < $5k.

## Actual edge (audited from resume, 2026-07-15)
The starting premise changed once we looked at the resume. Måns has **no consulting experience yet** — so pricing/monetization is a tool to sharpen at SK over autumn, *not* a foundation. His proven, repeatable edges are:
1. **Near-zero-cost customer acquisition** — Cysel: 850k monthly exposures at €0 ad spend (SEO); Holmstrand Bil +80% organic; early ventures 200k-follower network, 20M+ reach. Repeatable across 4 projects. **This is the moat** — CAC kills <$5k part-time businesses, and he's solved it for free, repeatedly.
2. **Operator in e-commerce + creator/social markets** — not hypotheses; he's built in both.
3. **Spotting structural gaps** — Cysel found and exploited a dead government API (Arbetsförmedlingen). Literally the market-hunt skill.
4. **Investment-analyst pattern-matching** across many business models (Round One Ventures) — good for killing ideas fast.

**Working wedge:** find an overlooked opening and get customers to it for near-zero cost, in e-commerce/creator markets he already understands. Pricing/monetization layered on top later.

## Kill-criteria (every idea must pass ALL of these)
1. **Reachable buyer** without enterprise sales — SMB, founder, creator, or self-serve. (Nights/weekends can't run a 6-month sales cycle.)
2. **Real willingness-to-pay** — a small buyer pays willingly, not "nice to have."
3. **Buildable and maintainable part-time** — deep code is fine (AI-assisted), but defensibility must come from distribution/data/domain, never the code itself.
4. **Async delivery** — value doesn't depend on Måns being available 9–5.
5. **Validatable for < $5k.**
6. **Frequent + urgent pain** — recurring, not once-a-year.

## The honest tension
We picked "start from the commercial edge," but Måns has no consulting experience yet — so that edge doesn't exist to stand on. The res-audited edge (cheap distribution + e-commerce/creator domain) is stronger *and* real, so we re-anchor on it. Pricing becomes the value-capture layer we add once SK sharpens it, not the wedge itself.

## Method — phases
- **Phase 0 — Sharpen the hunting ground (1–2 wks):** define 3–5 candidate "arenas" (specific buyer × specific monetization pain). *We are here.*
- **Phase 1 — Problem discovery:** talk to 15–25 real buyers. Kill arenas fast. Output: one validated pain.
- **Phase 2 — Willingness-to-pay:** design the smallest wedge; pre-sell before building. Output: signed intent / pre-payments.
- **Phase 3 — MVP:** vibe-code it, get first paying users.
- **Phase 4 — Decide:** venture vs side-hustle, based on the traction signal.

## Candidate arenas (v0 hypotheses to ATTACK, not answers)
Ranked by fit with the kill-criteria. All are guesses until we talk to people.
1. **E-commerce / Shopify sellers** — pricing, bundling, discount strategy is broken for small stores; huge reachable population; self-serve app-store distribution. *Risk: race-to-bottom, app saturation.*
2. **Creators / course & info-product sellers** — packaging and pricing is a mess, they leave real money on the table, will pay for revenue lift. *Risk: fickle, churny.*
3. **Indie / small SaaS founders** — price by gut, leak revenue, can't afford SK. *Risk: cheap, low WTP, drowning in free advice content.*
4. **Local service businesses (trades, clinics, gyms)** — systematically underprice, big money at stake. *Risk: hard to reach, offline, non-technical — worst accessibility.*

## Rules of engagement
- Bias to talking to customers over analysis. Rigor is not a substitute for a paying customer.
- Every arena gets killed or advanced on evidence, fast.
- Måns's SK knowledge deepens over the autumn — the edge compounds; we don't need to claim deep authority on day one.
