# Desk Research — Round 9: Food-Hygiene Deep-Dive + Elderly Care (2026-07-15)

## Food hygiene — the gap is CONFIRMED
- **Sweden has no national mandatory food-hygiene rating/publication system.** A proposition (2005/06:214) existed; never became a national consumer scheme. Publishing is **voluntary and fragmented**: Enköping runs a "Smiley", Malmö publishes godkänd/underkänd for some, Stockholm has the "Livsmedelskollen" app. Most of 290 kommuner do little/nothing centralized.
- **No national consumer aggregator exists.** Journalists build ad-hoc "matsnuskligan" lists from the data (SVT) — proof of public interest, no product.
- **Adjacent player to watch:** Eatie app aggregates 2.8M restaurant *reviews* for 16,000 restaurants — but that's reviews, NOT hygiene inspection data. The hygiene-data niche is still open (though Eatie could bolt it on).

## Data feasibility — workable, and the hard part IS the moat
- Big cities already publish: Stockholm (Livsmedelskollen app), Malmö (lists), Göteborg (partial). Inspection volumes are real (2024: Stockholm 8,005; Göteborg 4,700; Malmö 2,600).
- The rest: obtainable via offentlighetsprincipen (records requests) — laborious.
- **The real challenge = normalization.** Kommuner use different formats (avvikelser vs godkänd/underkänd vs smiley), unlike UK's uniform 0–5. Turning 290 inconsistent sources into one comparable rating is the hard, defensible work. That difficulty is exactly why no one's done it — and why it's a moat once done.

## Elderly care — attackable but weaker fit
- Incumbents: Äldreguiden (Socialstyrelsen, official, survey-based), Seniorval, Patientguiden (review-based).
- **Key difference: the data is CENTRALIZED** (Socialstyrelsen surveys) — so there's no data-collection moat; incumbents already have it. You'd compete only on UX/SEO, not on proprietary data.
- Verdict: lower priority than food hygiene, precisely because the moat (fragmented-data aggregation) is missing.

## "Attack several" — yes, but SEQUENCE it
The playbook (public data → normalize → SEO site → ads/leads) is genuinely repeatable, and the infrastructure (municipal-data scraping, SEO templates, utgivningsbevis, ad stack) is **reusable across verticals**. That's the real portfolio logic. But nail ONE first (food hygiene) to prove data-collection + ranking + monetization. Running several from day one = learn nothing well. Build the machine once, then replicate.
