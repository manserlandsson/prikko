## The wedge
**A lightweight true-cost + job-profitability tool for small Swedish hauliers (1–10 trucks), entering on the pricing/margin angle, expandable into planning.**

Why it's real (not a lead magnet):
- Solves a quantified problem they bleed money on: they take loss-making jobs blind. Over half struggle with profitability.
- Underserved band: big TMS/ERP (Opter, Monitor, etc.) built for large fleets — too heavy/expensive; Excel too weak. The 1–10 truck operator is stranded in the middle (same pattern as manufacturers' "Excel-to-ERP gap").
- Måns's edge, twice: (1) distribution — reachable via Transportnytt, Tidningen PROFFS, Svensk Åkeritidning, SÅ, FB groups; (2) pricing/margin is the exact Simon-Kucher competence, applied in a vertical where no one applies it.
- Buildable incrementally (vibe-code): start as a true-cost/per-job margin tool, grow into planning/admin.

## Honest risks
- Small hauliers are price-sensitive, not highly digital, and skeptical of "another admin tool."
- Mid-market TMS could move down-market.
- Adoption/trust in a low-margin, old-school industry is hard — must prove ROI in kronor fast.

## No-phone validation path
1. Publish a sharp Swedish content piece / landing page framing the exact problem ("vet du vilka körningar som faktiskt går med vinst?") + the tool concept.
2. Seed it into the transport FB groups + SEO for high-intent terms.
3. Measure: signups / waitlist / pre-orders. Demand before build.
4. If demand: build the MVP true-cost tool; get first paying åkeri.
