# Desk Research — Round 8: SEO Data-Aggregator Plays (2026-07-15)

Model: SEO-driven aggregator on public/open data (the Cysel pattern). Måns's reframe: **crowded is fine** — Cysel beat Monster/Indeed/Platsbanken on SEO. Filter = (a) winnable on SEO/UX, (b) ideally a data-collection moat (data others can't easily replicate).

## Candidate plays (ranked)
### 1. Swedish food-hygiene ratings site — LEAD (strongest Cysel fit)
- **The gap:** food-inspection results (livsmedelskontroll) are PUBLIC but fragmented across **290 municipalities**, each publishing separately. **No national consumer-facing aggregator exists in Sweden.**
- **Proven abroad:** UK's FSA Food Hygiene Rating Scheme + FoodHygieneCheck.com; Denmark's Smiley. Both successful, high-traffic, drive real consumer behavior. Sweden has proposed a national system since a 2005 SOU — never delivered.
- **Moat = the data labor.** Aggregating 290 kommuners' reports (some via offentlighetsprincipen requests) is hard — which is exactly why it doesn't exist and why it's defensible once done. Matches Måns's "hämta datan själv/manuellt och ge värde."
- **Search intent:** high and evergreen — "[restaurang] hygien", "livsmedelskontroll [stad]", "bäst/sämst restaurang hygien".
- **Monetization:** display ads, restaurant "claim/manage your profile" + reputation upsell, affiliate (bokning), later B2B data licensing.
- **Honest risks:** (1) government could finally launch a national site (tail risk — they've dithered 20 yrs); (2) data acquisition is laborious across 290 kommuner (start with big cities); (3) publishing named businesses' results → get an *utgivningsbevis* (like Ratsit/Hitta) for constitutional protection; restaurants may push back.

### 2. Elderly-care comparison (äldreboende/hemtjänst) — SERVED but beatable
Incumbents: Socialstyrelsen Äldreguiden (official, clunky), Seniorval, Patientguiden (4,007 hemtjänst, review-based). High-stakes emotional search; data across IVO + kommuner. Winnable on SEO/UX but not a clean gap. Medium.

### 3. Company/financial info — structural opening, entrenched incumbents
Bolagsverket + SCB "värdefulla datamängder" (company names, addresses, accounts, årsredovisningar) became **free on 3 Feb 2025**. Real enabler, but Allabolag/Ratsit/Proff/Merinfo/Hitta dominate SEO. Only viable with a sharp niche angle (e.g., a specific B2B prospecting slice). Medium.

### 4. Niche vertical job board (JobTech open API) — crowded, been-there
Arbetsförmedlingen's open Job Search/Job Stream APIs are rich (jobs, occupations, skills, forecasts, training). Generic Indeed-clone = no. A hyper-niche vertical board or a salary/skills/career-data tool could work. Lower priority.

## The throughline pattern
Fragmented municipal/public data (290 kommuner) that no one has centralized = repeatable Cysel-style opening. Food hygiene is the sharpest instance; others exist (bygglov/planned construction leads, environmental/tillsyn reports, care quality).

## Recommendation + next step
Lead with the **food-hygiene site**. Validate before building:
1. Confirm no strong Swedish incumbent (dedicated competitor scan).
2. Size SEO demand (search volumes for hygiene/restaurant + city queries).
3. Test data feasibility on ONE city (can we get the reports cleanly?).
4. Check utgivningsbevis path + any legal constraints.
5. Build a single-city MVP, measure organic traffic. Expand city by city.
