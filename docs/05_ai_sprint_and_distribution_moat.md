# Desk Research — Round 5: AI Sprint + The Distribution Moat (2026-07-15)

## Finding: the AI-rebuild lane is ALSO crowding fast
The obvious AI wedges in Sweden are already being built:
- **Offert/anbud for hantverkare:** Svensk Offertpartner, Offertio, Maisan, Next, Exayard, Billy.
- **Offentlig upphandling:** Tendium (funded leader), Tendoer, Clira, Anbudsintelligens.
- **Accounting / årsredovisning / protokoll:** Fortnox AI modules, Redofy, plus 15+ generic SMB AI tools (Klang.ai, satori, etc.).

Everyone can see the obvious AI workflows, so everyone is building them. Entering mid-2026, "AI rebuilds workflow X" is not, by itself, differentiated.

## The insight that actually matters
**In 2026 the AI tool is a commodity. Building it is not the moat — everyone can and does. The only durable edge is DISTRIBUTION.**
- These AI startups compete on features and often sales-led GTM. Almost none has a distribution/SEO moat.
- Måns's proven, repeatable edge IS distribution: Cysel 850k monthly exposures at €0; Holmstrand +80% organic; 200k-follower network.
- Conclusion: don't win by building a better tool. Win by **owning the audience** and attaching a (commodity) tool to it.

## Refined strategy: distribution-first
1. Pick ONE vertical "world."
2. Build a Swedish-language content/SEO asset + audience in it (Måns's edge, no phone, <$5k).
3. Monetize once attention is owned: AI tool, lead-gen, affiliate, or productized service — decided later.
The tool is downstream. The audience is the moat.

## The load-bearing decision we can no longer defer
A distribution/content moat only works if Måns will *sustainably create content* in the vertical. That requires genuine affinity. This is the exact "what world could you live in for 2+ years?" question from day one — deflected then, binding now. Everything downstream (niche, content, monetization) depends on it.

## Next step
Måns picks the vertical "world." Then: run a distribution-feasibility scan (can we rank in Swedish for this vertical's terms? where's the audience? what's the cheapest attention channel?) and design the first no-phone content probe.
