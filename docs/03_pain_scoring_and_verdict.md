# Desk Research — Round 3: Scored Pain + Honest Verdict (2026-07-15)

Method: frequency × emotional intensity scoring on mined complaints (subreddits, Capterra low-star reviews, operator blogs). Goal = the exact payable sentence.

## Permit / inspection tracking — pain table
| Pain (operator's words) | Frequency | Intensity | Already solved by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Checking many AHJ portals manually eats 2.5–5 hrs/week | High | High | PermitFlow, InstaPermit, Shovels.ai |
| Permit expired unnoticed → $5–15k reinstatement + delay | Medium | Very High | ExpiryEdge, PermitGuard, PermitNerd |
| Missed/failed inspection → crew shows up, can't pour | Medium | High | Partially (SignedOff, field tools) |
| Separate login per city, can't share access | High | Medium | Structural; unsolved but hard |

**Verdict: pain is REAL and quantified — but the wedge is contested and technically heavy.**
- Competitors already here: PermitFlow (funded), Shovels.ai, InstaPermit, SignedOff, PermitNerd, PermitGuard, ExpiryEdge, PermitDesk + enterprise gov (Tyler/EnerGov, OpenGov, MyGov).
- The sharpest sub-pain (portal auto-monitoring) needs scraping/integrating dozens of brittle gov portals — not a solo weekend build.
- US-only construct (AHJ). Nordic bygglov is a *different* pain, not a localization of this one.
- **Conclusion: too late + too heavy for a no-phone solo entering now. Don't fall in love with it.**

## Runner-up: accounting micro-workflows
Real, universal pain (bank-statement data entry, dunning) — but heavily automated already, crowded, horizontal. Not a differentiated solo bet.

## The real takeaway (this is the win)
The **method works**: in one afternoon of desk research we validated a pain's size AND discovered it's already contested — which would have cost months to learn by building. The pattern (boring niche, one workflow, SEO, no phone) is right. We just need to point the proven engine at a niche that is NOT already swarmed.

## The fork we haven't resolved: geography
This decides which niches are even viable and changes the whole competition picture:
- **English-language / global self-serve (SEO):** biggest market, plays to Måns's SEO edge — but fierce competition, and he's not embedded in the US.
- **Nordic-language (Swedish first):** smaller market, but a real language/localization moat, far less competition, and self-serve SEO still works in Swedish. The Cysel-style opening.

## Next step
Resolve geography, then re-run the frequency × intensity engine on 2–3 *less obvious, less swarmed* niches in that market. Kill fast, keep the one with high pain + low competition + self-serve fit.
